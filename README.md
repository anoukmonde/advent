Tasman Advent Calendar

A field atlas of 24 specimens from Aotearoa New Zealand and Australia, one per day, 1 to 24 December 2026.

Each door opens to a hand-drawn plate that comes alive: the record spins at a scientifically accurate 33⅓ rpm, the platypus waddles (and glows under UV, as platypuses do), the panther's eyes glow, and the emu leaves the plate at roughly 50 km/h.

Field notes
Doors unlock by date on the viewer's own device. Past doors stay open.
Videos play inside the doors when hosted on GitHub Pages.
Preview mode: tap the compass rose five times.
Chaos mode: hold the compass rose, or enter ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A on a keyboard. Every opened plate starts moving at once.
Reduced motion is respected: if your device asks for less animation, the specimens hold still.
Anatomy

One file, index.html. No build step, no dependencies, no frameworks. Sketches are inline SVG with a turbulence filter for the hand-inked wobble, animated with CSS.
